Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our software development process and partnership models.

What industries do you specialize in? expand_more
We specialize in delivering high-impact solutions for Fintech, Healthcare, E-commerce, and Logistics. However, our team's deep expertise in AI and Cloud infrastructure allows us to tackle complex challenges across any data-driven industry.
How long does a typical project take? expand_more
Timeline varies based on complexity. An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) typically takes 8-12 weeks, while more complex enterprise-grade platforms can range from 4 to 9 months. We provide detailed milestones during our discovery phase.
Do you offer ongoing post-launch support? expand_more
Absolutely. We offer flexible maintenance and support packages that include security patches, performance monitoring, and iterative feature updates to ensure your product scales seamlessly with your business.
How do you handle project communication? expand_more
Transparency is our priority. We use Slack for daily communication, Jira for task tracking, and schedule weekly sync-up meetings via video call to review progress and gather feedback on the latest sprints.
